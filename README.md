# WantAce — Solutions Engineer Intern Assignment

This repository organizes the deliverables for a Solutions Engineer assignment: business workflow discovery, requirements analysis, data-quality review, account handoff, customer-facing content, and a product demo.

## Submission status

**This repository began as a publication scaffold. Do not treat every assignment deliverable as complete unless the supporting evidence has been added and checked.** In particular, real-business fieldwork, genuine owner quotations, permissioned photos, and the final demo recording must reflect work actually completed.

## Deliverables

### Part A — Business workflow audit

Document a real manufacturing, processing, or repair business. Trace an order from enquiry through production, quality checks, delivery, and payment. Record the tools actually used, the confirmed operational issue, two direct quotations, and evidence collected with permission. Do not invent observations or business metrics.

### Part B — Requirements for Vardhan Steelware

The working analysis focuses on order-status visibility and fragmented tracking. Data-quality items to verify against the original client pack include repeated-looking order rows, missing customer or rate information, payment holds, returns, partial dispatches, and outsourced processing quantities recorded in different units.

The proposed Phase 1 scope is a recommendation to confirm with the client:

- A central order register with unique order IDs and clear status ownership.
- Required-field validation and a history of status changes.
- Linked partial dispatch and return records.
- Outsourced processing batches with units and sent/received quantities.
- Payment-hold and missing-information flags.
- A mobile-friendly report for pending orders and unresolved exceptions.

Forecasting, broad ERP replacement, and complex integrations should wait until the core workflow and source data have been validated.

### Part C — Account Executive handoff

Summarize the confirmed business problem, current workflow, potential adoption risks, open questions, decision-maker, and next step. The suggested next step is a workflow walkthrough with the owner and the staff responsible for order coordination, production/dispatch, and accounts.

### Part D — LinkedIn post and Meta ad

Base customer-facing content on a verified finding from Part A. Keep the business anonymous and avoid presenting proposed benefits, hypothetical figures, or unverified observations as real outcomes.

### Part E — Demo and AI usage

Add the actual demo recording and supporting evidence required by the original brief. Keep the AI usage disclosure accurate and mark only checks that have actually been completed.

## Project files

- `index.html` — simple web page that summarizes the assignment deliverables.
- `AI_Usage_Note.txt` — AI usage disclosure that must be reviewed and updated to match the work actually done.
- `README.md` — requirements analysis, handoff notes, publishing guidance, and submission checklist.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Open the repository's **Settings → Pages**.
2. Select **Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` and `/(root)`, and save.
3. Wait for the Pages deployment to finish, then open the public URL in a private browser window.
4. Check every link and confirm that required evidence and the demo are included before sharing the submission.

## Final review

Before submitting, verify the real-business evidence, compare all client-pack details with their source, label assumptions as recommendations, remove unsupported claims, check the demo links, and ensure the AI usage note describes actual work accurately.
